PROJECT OVERVIEW:
SSS Auto Spares — Professional Business Website for Chennai-Based Auto Parts Dealer

CLIENT TYPE:
Local automotive spare parts business (B2B + B2C hybrid)

PROJECT DESCRIPTION:
Designed and developed a complete, production-ready business website for SSS Auto Spares, a Chennai-based auto parts dealer specializing in new spares, used parts, and vehicle buying services. The website serves as a digital catalog and lead generation platform, enabling customers to browse inventory, submit enquiries, and connect directly with the business.

KEY FEATURES DELIVERED:

1. CATALOG SHOWCASE SYSTEM:
   - Organized inventory display by category
   - New spares, OEM-grade parts, and used parts sections
   - Visual product cards with specifications
   - Search-friendly structure for easy navigation

2. ENQUIRY & LEAD GENERATION:
   - "Start an Enquiry" CTA system
   - Contact form integration
   - Direct WhatsApp/phone integration
   - Location-based contact display

3. TRUST & CREDIBILITY ELEMENTS:
   - "Why SSS Auto Spares" value proposition section
   - Direct sourcing network explanation
   - Transparent enquiry process highlights
   - Chennai-wide dispatch and pickup information
   - Customer testimonial/video section

4. SERVICE PRESENTATION:
   - Old car buying and selling services
   - Four-wheeler total loss vehicle purchasing
   - Genuine fitment guarantees (bumpers, fenders, doors, lighting)
   - Precision fit and bench-tested quality assurance

5. LOCAL SEO OPTIMIZATION:
   - Chennai-specific keywords and location targeting
   - Google Maps integration
   - Local business schema markup
   - Mobile-first responsive design

6. CONTENT MANAGEMENT:
